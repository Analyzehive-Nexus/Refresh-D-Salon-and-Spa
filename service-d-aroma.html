Got Questions

Frequently Asked

Everything you need to know before your visit — answered simply and clearly.

01 Do I need an appointment for an aromatherapy massage at Refresh D Thai Spa?
+

No prior appointment is mandatory — walk-ins are welcome and bookings are typically confirmed within 30 minutes. That said, we recommend reserving a slot in advance on weekends and public holidays, as our therapists fill quickly. Booking requires no prepayment and cancellation is always free.

02 Is aromatherapy massage safe if I have sensitive skin or allergies?
+

Yes, with a simple disclosure during your pre-session consultation. We use 100% certified organic essential oils — no synthetics, no harsh chemicals — with sweet almond as the base carrier. If you have a known allergy to any oil or tree nut, mention it during your consultation and we will substitute at no extra charge.

03 What is the difference between a 60-minute and 90-minute aromatherapy session?
+

The core ritual is the same across all durations — welcome tea, consultation, signature massage, and closing tea are all included. The difference is depth and coverage. A 60-minute session focuses on your primary tension areas: back, shoulders, and neck. The 90-minute session extends to the legs, feet, and arms for a full-body release. The 90-minute session is our most popular choice, especially for first-time guests.

04 How often should I get an aromatherapy massage for lasting results?
+

Once every two to four weeks is the recommended frequency for cumulative therapeutic benefit — stress reduction, improved sleep, and muscular recovery compound with regularity. For those managing chronic tension or high-pressure work, a bi-weekly session is often the turning point. A single session, however, produces immediate and noticeable effects on its own.

05 Can I book an aromatherapy massage without paying in advance?
+

Yes — no prepayment is required. You pay only after your session, in full, at the spa. Free cancellation applies to all bookings. Use code PTS10 at checkout for 10% off any session duration.

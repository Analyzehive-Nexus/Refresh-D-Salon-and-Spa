Got Questions

Frequently Asked

Everything you need to know before your visit — answered simply and clearly.

01 Do I need a prior appointment?
+

Walk-ins are welcome based on availability, but we strongly recommend booking ahead to guarantee your preferred time slot and therapist.

02 What should I wear during the massage?
+

We provide comfortable spa attire. For oil massages, you will be professionally draped at all times to ensure your privacy and comfort.

03 Is there a cancellation policy?
+

Free cancellation up to 2 hours before your appointment. No prepayment is required at the time of booking.

04 Can I request a specific therapist?
+

Yes! Mention your preferred therapist when booking and we will do our best to accommodate based on availability.

05 Are there health conditions to be aware of?
+

Certain conditions like skin infections or recent surgeries may be contraindicated. Please inform our team during consultation — we will find the safest treatment for you.

06 How do I apply the PTS10 discount code?
+

Enter PTS10 in the coupon field of the booking form to instantly save 10% on any session duration.

The Treatment

The Wave of Aloha.
Fluid Energy.

A sacred Hawaiian healing massage that uses continuous, fluid, and wave-like forearm strokes to dissolve physical blocks and restore energetic harmony.

Experience the spirit of Aloha with Lomi Lomi — a traditional Hawaiian bodywork that goes far beyond a typical massage. Known historically as 'loving hands', Lomi Lomi uses continuous, long, fluid strokes with the therapist's hands and forearms, simulating gentle ocean waves moving over the body. This rhythmic, sweeping movement allows the nervous system to fully let go, dissolving both chronic muscle tension and emotional blocks.

Your therapist works intuitively, combining long effleurage strokes with passive joint releases and gentle stretching to restore range of motion and harmony to the body. Using warm organic, virgin coconut oil, this treatment deeply nourishes and hydrates the skin while promoting a seamless energetic flow from head to toe.

Lomi Lomi does not simply treat localized tension; it treats the body as a whole, leaving you with a profound sense of completeness and deep, lasting peace.

Key benefits of this treatment:

✓

Virgin Coconut Oil — rich in fatty acids, deeply hydrates and protects the skin barrier

✓

Forearm Stroke Technique — covers larger areas of the body simultaneously for a fluid, wave-like sensation

✓

Passive Joint Release — gently releases tension held deep in the joints and restores mobility

✓

Aloha Spirit — a holistic approach that honors the connection between mind, body, and spirit

+

Walk-ins are welcome based on availability, but we strongly recommend booking ahead to guarantee your preferred time slot and therapist.

02 What should I wear during the massage?
+

We provide comfortable spa attire. For oil massages, you will be professionally draped at all times to ensure your privacy and comfort.

+

Free cancellation up to 2 hours before your appointment. No prepayment is required at the time of booking.
